# Research notes (web search, 24 Oct 2026)

No public record of either hackathon case as written. Related material found:

## Territory first, then site (supports our allocation logic)
- Franchise cannibalisation typically starts when a brand opens wherever a good site appears without designing territories first; a high-scoring site does not matter in a saturated territory. https://growthfactor.ai/resources/blog/franchise-territory-design-cannibalization
- Cannibalisation tolerance should be set before reviewing any site. https://www.maptive.com/prevent-franchise-cannibalization/
- CKE checks a proposed site falls inside its assigned territory and is not too close to an existing location. https://www.caliper.com/map-software/case-studies/how-cke-grew-their-business-with-maptitude.htm
- Indian vendors sell pincode-level cannibalisation flags. https://analytics.zoop.one/industries/retail.html

## Partner selection
- Journal of Services Marketing (2011), master franchises: use clearly defined selection criteria and a defined process. https://emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/08876041111149694/full/html

## Threshold anchors (third-party and unofficial: call them estimates in the deck)
- Typically quoted for a Lenskart-type store: ₹25 to 35 lakh total investment, 300 to 500 sq ft, claimed payback 12 to 18 months. https://payhip.com/tasknetworkx/blog/news/complete-guide-to-cost-profit-process-0c6p
- Franchise India listing: ₹20 to 30 lakh, 300 to 500 sq ft. https://www.franchiseindia.com/business-opportunities/opticians-eye-wear-in-india/ssc-246/range-3000000-10000000
- Minimum area near 250 sq ft. https://techstory.in/?p=438943

## Onboarding
- Franchise onboarding portals typically combine checklists, training schedules and milestone tracking. https://www.taskade.com/templates/customer-onboarding-portals/franchise-location-onboarding-portal
- A vendor blog claims structured onboarding raises franchisee retention by up to 30%. This is a marketing claim: do not use it as evidence. https://wptesting.authbridge.com/?p=82589

## Our threshold choices (all editable in the app, all to be defended on slide 4)
Minimum investment ₹30 lakh (Tier 1), ₹25 lakh (Tier 2), ₹20 lakh (Tier 3); hold between 70% and 100% of the floor, reject below 70%; minimum space 250 sq ft, ideal 300 to 500; upper floors held; earliest realistic opening 10 weeks (plus 6 if no space yet); first-timers (under 1 year) get the extended training track.
