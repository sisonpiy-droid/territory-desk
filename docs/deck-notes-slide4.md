# Slide 4: assumptions and how we would check them

Numbers below are from the judges'-layout workbook (20 applications, 9 territories). All of the assumptions can be changed in the app (Assumptions tab and the Before and after panel), and every decision updates straight away.

| Assumption | Our value | Where it comes from | How we would check it in a pilot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum investment | Rs 30 / 25 / 20 lakh (Tier 1 / 2 / 3) | Public figures for a Lenskart-type store: Rs 20 to 35 lakh (research notes) | Compare with the brand's real fit-out cost for the last 10 stores |
| Hold, not reject, between 70% and 100% of the floor | 70% | Our call: a co-investor can close a small gap | Count how many held applicants close the gap within 30 days |
| Space | 250 sq ft minimum, 300 to 500 ideal; ground floor or mall | Public listings (250 to 500 sq ft) | Store sales by size and floor from the brand's own stores |
| Earliest opening | 10 weeks (+6 with no space yet) | Sum of the onboarding steps | Time each step for the first 5 pilot stores |
| One store per pin code; pin-number distance as a stand-in for catchment | Brief rule; 5 / 25 pin numbers | Brief; a rough proxy | Replace with catchment overlap from the brand's sales data |
| Manual effort | 20 min to screen, 5 per pin check, 15 per follow-up | Our estimate, editable on screen | Time five real applications with the expansion team before the pilot |

## Stress test: what if we got a threshold wrong?
We move each threshold 10% up and down and see which decisions flip (Scenarios tab).
- Only 4 of 20 decisions flip: APP-001, APP-019 and APP-010 sit right at the Rs 25 lakh Tier 2 floor, and APP-018 (Rs 21 lakh, 260 sq ft) is close to the Tier 3 floor and the space minimum.
- The other 16 hold under every single 10% change, so the thresholds are not doing all the work.
- Fragile decisions are marked low confidence and listed for a person to check before anything is signed.
- "Every threshold 10% stricter" takes approvals from 8 to 7 and holds from 8 to 10; we can show this side by side live.

## Confidence on screen
- High: 10+ points clear of the next rival, or decided on facts in the form.
- Medium: 3 to 10 points clear, or a data flag (for example, funding depends on a loan).
- Low: under 3 points clear, or fragile.
- On the judges' layout: 12 high, 4 medium, 4 low.
